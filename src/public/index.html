Scores

Each rubric was answered independently against this transcript. Yes/no rubrics report a probability — 0.99 is a confident yes, 0.04 a confident no, and anything near 0.50 means the model could not tell. Score and option rubrics report their answer plus a separate confidence, which reflects how concentrated the model’s distribution was. Low confidence is not a bad score — it usually means this conversation did not contain enough to judge, and the session is flagged for a human to read.
